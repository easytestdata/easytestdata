Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

This policy covers the easytestdata.com website and EasyTestData Cloud. Copies of EasyTestData that you run yourself (for example with npx easytestdata ui on your own computer) send nothing to us and are not covered here. The playground generates data entirely in your browser; nothing you generate there is sent to us.

EasyTestData is free. We do not sell anything, do not collect payment information, and do not run product analytics or advertising trackers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • Monitor service health and debug issues
  • Enforce usage limits and prevent abuse

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on secure infrastructure. Security measures include:

  • QBO OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 with unique initialization vectors per record
  • All connections use TLS/HTTPS
  • JWT tokens with short expiry (15 minutes) and rotating refresh tokens

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to persist authentication tokens (JWT access and refresh tokens). We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Kept while your account exists, and deleted when you ask us to delete your account
  • Job artifacts (generated files): Deleted automatically about a week after the job finishes
  • QBO tokens: Deleted immediately when a connection is removed
  • Logs and error reports: Kept only as long as needed for security and debugging

7. Your Rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data. The web app lets you remove QBO connections yourself (finished jobs and their downloads expire on their own, see above); for everything else, including correcting your account details, exporting your data or deleting your whole account, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will do it for you.
