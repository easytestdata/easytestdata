Load this into your QBO sandbox

The same template, scenario and seed produce the same books from the CLI. Connect a sandbox once with npx easytestdata auth (you'll need a free Intuit developer account and sandbox company), then run:

{{PG_COMMAND}}

Every record is tagged EZTD, so npx easytestdata purge --mode generated clears it again. Purge deletes the transactions it added, makes its customers, vendors, employees and items inactive (accounts stay), and never touches records you made by hand (details). Sandbox companies only.

CLI docs Run it locally Use EasyTestData Cloud

All data is synthetic: names, addresses, emails and phone numbers are made up, and every record is tagged EZTD so it can be purged. Revenue and expenses are allocated to hit the scenario's targets exactly; the profit shown is that target (why QuickBooks' P&L differs).
